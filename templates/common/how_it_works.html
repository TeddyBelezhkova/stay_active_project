🏃‍♂️ How Stay Active Works

Stay Active is a corporate activity tracker rewarding employee wellness, consistency, and athletic milestones.

1. Create Your Profile & Log Workouts

Participants log daily workouts (running, cycling, swimming, gym) along with proof photos of their achievements.

2. Manual Verification & Review

Submitted logs are reviewed by team admins. Public workout proofs are published to the Wall of Fame for social encouragement.

3. Earn Badges & Collect Trophies

When participants meet specific milestones (e.g., 100 KM Club, Rain or Shine runner), admins award corresponding milestone badges.

4. Climb the Corporate Leaderboard

Earned badges and completed activities update employee rankings in real-time on the Homepage Leaderboard!
