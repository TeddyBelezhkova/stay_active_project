🏃 How Stay Active Works

Stay Active is a corporate activity tracker rewarding employee wellness, consistency, and athletic milestones.

1. Create Your Profile & Log Workouts

Log daily workouts (running, cycling, swimming, gym) along with workout notes and optional proof photos.

2. Manual Verification & Review

Submitted entries are verified by team leads. Public workout proofs appear on the Wall of Fame for social encouragement.

3. Earn Badges & Collect Trophies

Meet distance and endurance criteria to collect 15 custom milestone badges in your personal trophy cabinet.

4. Climb the Corporate Leaderboard

Earned points and activities push you and your department to the top of the live workspace ranking!
